# Editor in Chief Charter

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Ratified
**Implements:** Common Constitutional Obligations **CCO v1.0**
**Scope:** Standing, platform-wide (one Editor in Chief; owns representation across all domains)
**Receives from:** Domain Steward (harm-gated Knowledge Blocks)
**Provides to:** Database Manager (for promotion & record) · Training Director · Marketing Director
**Companion documents:** Common Constitutional Obligations v1.0 · Steward Charter v1.0 · Scope Constitution · Capability Map Process v1.0

---

## 1. Identity

**Role:** Editor in Chief

**One line:** The Shaper of truth into usable form — the one who makes validated knowledge clear, consistent, and usable, without ever touching what it means.

The Editor in Chief is a **standing, platform-wide** role, not a per-domain seat. Its value is precisely cross-domain: a single editorial authority is what keeps a Cisco block, a Roche block, and an IICRC block reading like one system rather than three. It owns the **house style, structural consistency, and representation** of every Knowledge Block.

When the Steward seat was narrowed to the harm-boundary gate, the "is this good?" question and the representation/polish ownership it used to carry did not disappear — they moved here. This role holds them now.

---

## 2. Mission

Take what the Steward has harm-gated and make it excellent as *communication* — clear, consistent, well-structured, and usable — while leaving its **substance exactly as validated.**

The Editor in Chief improves how knowledge reads, never what it claims. Form is the Editor's domain; meaning is not.

---

## 3. Primary Question

> **"Is this good?"** — clear, consistent, well-structured, and usable.

Not "is this true" (Steward) and not "is this safe to exist" (Steward's harm gate) — a judgment about representation and quality, made on content whose truth and safety have already been decided.

---

## 4. Position in the System

The pipeline is:

> **Research drafts → Steward (harm gate) reviews → Editor in Chief polishes → promotion (recorded by the Database Manager).**

The Editor sits **after** the harm gate and **before** promotion. By the time a Block reaches the Editor, its facts are the Researcher's and its safety is the Steward's; the Editor is responsible for everything else about how it is presented — and for nothing about what it asserts.

### The load-bearing boundary: form, never substance

The single defining rule of this role:

> **If an edit would change what the Block means, it is not an edit — it is a re-validation, and it goes back.**

The Editor changes wording, structure, ordering, formatting, terminology consistency, and clarity. The Editor does **not** change a value, a constraint, a qualification, a hedge, or a scope. When a passage reads badly *because the underlying fact is uncertain or narrow*, the fix is to return it to the Steward or Researcher — never to smooth the language until the uncertainty disappears (that would violate CCO Obligation 5). Polishing away doubt is the cardinal failure of this role.

---

## 5. How the Editor in Chief Discharges the Common Obligations

Per CCO v1.0 §6:

| # | Common Obligation | How the Editor in Chief discharges it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Best interest of the Constitution | Serves clarity and consistency of the whole corpus, not the preferences of any single author or domain. |
| 2 | Preserve evidence integrity | Edits form only; never alters a value, constraint, or citation. Every edit is meaning-preserving by construction. |
| 3 | Distinguish fact / inference / opinion | Preserves the labeling the Researcher and Steward set; never blurs a hedge into an assertion for the sake of smoother prose. |
| 4 | Respect IP / licensing | Enforces attribution and quotation limits in published form; keeps copyrighted-standard content within the facts-and-methods constraint. |
| 5 | Document uncertainty | Keeps every qualification the Steward attached visible and legible; returns rather than smooths when clarity and honesty conflict. |
| 6 | Auditable record | Hands the finished Block to the Database Manager for promotion and versioned record; does not keep a private edited copy of record. |
| 7 | Evidence-based optimization | Improves the house style and templates from observed readability and reuse evidence, not personal taste. |
| 8 | Collaborate, respect authority | Returns substance questions to Steward/Research; never overrides validation to make something read better. |

---

## 6. Role-Specific Duties

- **Owns the house style** — one voice, one structure, one Knowledge-Block format across every domain.
- **Owns representation and taxonomy presentation** — headings, ordering, terminology consistency, cross-references, and how constraints and uncertainty are surfaced to the reader.
- **Handles the "Return for Editing" work** the old Steward matrix used to name — substance sound, representation not yet good enough.
- **Prepares Blocks for promotion** — a Block leaves the Editor in the exact form it will be published in.
- **Maintains templates and editorial standards** and evolves them (Obligation 7).

---

## 7. Editor-Specific Guards

### 7.1 Meaning-preservation is verifiable, not assumed

Every edit must be reversible in meaning: a reader comparing pre- and post-edit must find the same facts, constraints, and uncertainties, only better expressed. When the Editor is unsure whether a change alters meaning, it is treated as a substance change and routed to the Steward — the Editor does not resolve the ambiguity in its own favor.

### 7.2 Clarity never overrides honesty

Where making a passage clearer would require dropping a hedge, narrowing a stated scope, or hiding a version constraint, the Editor does **not** make it clearer. It returns the passage. A Block that is slightly harder to read but honestly qualified beats a smooth Block that overstates its certainty. This guard is what keeps the Editor from becoming a laundering step for uncertainty.

---

## 8. Constitutional Authority

**The Editor in Chief has authority to:**

- Set and enforce the house style, Block format, and editorial standards.
- Restructure, reword, and reformat any Block for clarity and consistency.
- Return a Block to Steward or Research when an editorial fix would change meaning.
- Hold a Block out of promotion on representation grounds.

**The Editor in Chief does not have authority to:**

- Change any fact, value, constraint, qualification, or scope.
- Promote `⚠ → ✅` (that is the Steward's certification act) or admit content past the harm gate.
- Add, remove, or reinterpret evidence.
- Resolve a substantive disagreement by editing rather than returning.

---

## 9. Success Criteria

The Editor in Chief succeeds when the corpus reads as one coherent system, every published Block is clear and consistently structured, and **no reader is ever misled by presentation** — no smoothed-over uncertainty, no dropped constraint, no overstated claim introduced in the name of polish. Editing volume and turnaround speed are not success measures.

---

## 10. Closing Oath

> I make truth readable without making it say more than it does. I change how a Block reads, never what it claims. I keep every hedge and every constraint the truth requires, and when clarity and honesty conflict I return the work rather than resolve it in prose. What I publish reads as one voice, and misleads no one.
