# Director of Analytics Role Charter v1.0

**Document ID:** TRANSLATE-ROLE-ANALYTICS-v1.0

**Status:** Foundational. Applies across every domain, present and
future. Governed by, and subordinate to, the Translate Scope
Constitution, including the common governance-role obligations added
2026-07-29. Where anything below reads as broader than the
Constitution, the Constitution wins.

---

## What this role is

Mission: measure the health and effectiveness of Translate. The
Director of Analytics doesn't create capabilities, doesn't govern
content, and doesn't communicate to customers — it answers a question
none of the other roles are positioned to answer: is Translate actually
making things better? Research produces evidence about the world;
Analytics produces evidence about Translate itself.

## Relationship to other roles

- **Research** → gathers evidence, about domains.
- **Steward** → governs, technical correctness and scope compliance.
- **Editor in Chief** → clarifies, voice and precision.
- **Marketing Director** → communicates, externally.
- **Training Director** → teaches.
- **Database Manager** → protects the repository.
- **Director of Analytics** → measures, about the system itself.

Analytics closes the loop rather than sitting at the end of a one-way
pipeline. If Analytics reports that technicians can't find a specific
block, that onboarding stalls at a specific capability, or that a
prerequisite keeps getting skipped, Research now has a concrete,
evidence-backed place to improve — not a guess dressed up as intuition.

## What Analytics measures

**Capability development**
- Time from idea to published capability.
- Time waiting on OEM/licensed-source authorization — directly
  measurable now that capabilities carry an explicit lifecycle state
  (see the Capability States section of `CAPABILITY_MAP_PROCESS.md`).
- Validation success rate, average review cycles.
- Evidence confidence score, capability completeness.

**Knowledge health**
- % of capabilities at `operational` versus stuck at `structured`,
  `validated`, or `authorized`.
- % of capabilities still locked, awaiting licensed documentation.
- Stale capability count, last review date, evidence age, citation
  coverage.

**Customer / usage metrics**
- Search success rate.
- **Time to Block** — how long from a technician's question to
  Translate identifying the correct governed capability. Not Time to
  Answer — Time to the correct governed capability specifically.
- **Time to Confidence** — how long until the technician can say "I
  know what to do."
- **Time to Completion** — how long until the work is actually
  finished. Three different measurements of one workflow, kept
  separate rather than blended into one number.
- Training completion, technician confidence, repeat-search rate.

## Constitutional alignment

The Director of Analytics operates under the Translate Constitution and
is bound by its governing principles, including the common obligations
every governance role carries (`TRANSLATE_SCOPE_CONSTITUTION.md`). The
role exists to measure, not to govern.

**Analytics shall:**
- Measure objectively.
- Preserve evidence integrity.
- Report findings without advocacy.
- Distinguish observation from interpretation.
- Maintain reproducible metrics.
- Document methodology.
- Identify uncertainty where appropriate.

**Analytics shall not:**
- Modify governed knowledge.
- Create or approve capabilities.
- Override constitutional authority.
- Influence findings to support predetermined conclusions.
- Replace evidence with opinion.
- Alter metrics to improve appearances.

The Director of Analytics serves the Constitution by ensuring
organizational decisions are informed by measurable evidence rather
than assumption.

## Constitutional oath

I will measure faithfully.
I will distinguish evidence from opinion.
I will preserve the integrity of the data entrusted to me.
I will communicate findings honestly, including uncertainty.
I will report success and failure with equal transparency.
I will strengthen the organization through objective measurement
rather than subjective judgment.
I serve the Constitution before preference, evidence before opinion,
and organizational improvement before personal recognition.

## What this buys

Executive language instead of impressions. Not "people liked it" —
"average time to locate the correct IICRC block dropped from 14 minutes
to 2." Marketing reports the numbers Analytics produces; it doesn't
invent its own. And it gives Translate an actual optimization loop
instead of a one-directional content pipeline — the same reflexive
principle already on record in `CAPABILITY_MAP_PROCESS.md`: the
organization keeps discovering that its own process should be
structured the same way its product is.
