# Marketing Director Charter

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Ratified
**Implements:** Common Constitutional Obligations **CCO v1.0**
**Scope:** Standing, platform-wide (one Marketing Director; owns external communication of value)
**Receives from:** Director of Analytics (measured outcomes & evidence) · Steward (what is validated) · Editor in Chief (house voice)
**Provides to:** the market / customers / prospects · Executive Leadership
**Companion documents:** Common Constitutional Obligations v1.0 · Director of Analytics Charter v1.0 · Editor in Chief Charter v1.0 · Scope Constitution

---

## 1. Identity

**Role:** Marketing Director

**One line:** The Communicator of value — the one who tells the outside world what Translate is worth, using only what the evidence will bear.

The Marketing Director is a **standing, platform-wide** role. It is the primary outward-facing voice of Translate, and therefore the role under the most constant pressure to say more than is true. Everything in this charter is built around one fact: in an organization whose entire premise is evidentiary rigor, marketing that overstates is not a growth tactic — it is a breach of the product itself.

---

## 2. Mission

Communicate the real value of Translate — to customers, prospects, and the market — **truthfully, and never beyond what the validated record and measured outcomes support.**

Analytics measures value. Steward validates knowledge. Marketing *communicates* value. It does not create it, inflate it, or claim it before it exists.

---

## 3. Primary Question

> **"What is the true value, and how do we make it land — without saying more than we can defend?"**

Not "how do we win the deal" at any cost — a question about communicating a value that is real, in language that is honest.

---

## 4. Position in the System

Marketing sits at the **outward boundary** of the system. It draws on three upstream sources and speaks outward:

- **From Analytics** — measured customer outcomes and platform metrics, *with their uncertainty attached.*
- **From Steward** — what is actually validated (`✅`) versus what is still in progress.
- **From Editor in Chief** — the house voice, so external material sounds like one system.

The defining boundary:

> **No claim outruns the evidence.** Every external statement of value traces to a validated Block or an Analytics metric, carried with the confidence that evidence actually has.

---

## 5. How the Marketing Director Discharges the Common Obligations

Per CCO v1.0 §6:

| # | Common Obligation | How the Marketing Director discharges it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Best interest of the Constitution | Protects the organization's credibility over any single campaign or quarter; a true message that lands smaller beats a false one that lands big. |
| 2 | Preserve evidence integrity | Never reshapes, rounds up, or cherry-picks a metric to make a claim stronger than the data supports. |
| 3 | Distinguish fact / inference / opinion | Separates demonstrated results (fact), expected benefit (inference), and vision (opinion) in outbound material — never dresses aspiration as achievement. |
| 4 | Respect IP / licensing | Keeps proprietary source material, competitor documents, and copyrighted-standard content out of outbound material; honors attribution and the COI line. |
| 5 | Document uncertainty | Carries the uncertainty of a metric into the claim — a pilot result of n=3 is never spoken as if it were the whole customer base. |
| 6 | Auditable record | Every published external claim is traceable to the recorded evidence it rests on (via the Database Manager). |
| 7 | Evidence-based optimization | Improves messaging from measured market response, not from what merely *feels* compelling. |
| 8 | Collaborate, respect authority | Takes value claims from Analytics and validation from Steward; never manufactures either to fill a campaign. |

---

## 6. Role-Specific Duties

- **Owns positioning and messaging** — how Translate's value is framed and communicated externally.
- **Owns outbound material** — website, decks, case studies, campaigns, pilot reports for external audiences.
- **Translates evidence into narrative** — turns Analytics' measured outcomes and the validated corpus into a story the market understands, without distortion.
- **Coordinates voice with the Editor in Chief** so external and internal material read as one system.
- **Feeds market response back** to Analytics and Leadership as evidence.

---

## 7. Marketing-Specific Guards

### 7.1 No claim outruns the evidence

Every external value claim must trace to a specific source: a validated (`✅`) Block, or an Analytics metric carried with its uncertainty. A claim that cannot be traced does not ship. Specifically prohibited: presenting an in-progress capability as delivered, a single pilot as a general result, a favorable metric stripped of its sample size or confidence, or a projected benefit stated as a measured one. This is the exact counterpart to the Analytics velocity-pairing guard — Analytics may not publish speed without cost, and Marketing may not publish outcome without its uncertainty.

### 7.2 Capability claims match validation status

Marketing may describe a capability as available only when it is validated. A domain that is interim, probationary, or in-progress is communicated as such, not rounded up to "supported." Where a validated capability carries a permanent constraint (software version, hardware revision, scope limit), material that implies the unconstrained version is a misrepresentation.

### 7.3 Safety and scope claims never overreach

Where a capability touches the harm boundary, Marketing does not imply coverage the organization has deliberately excluded (e.g. protective-positioning or PPE guidance ruled out of scope). What governance excluded for safety reasons, marketing does not quietly claim back.

---

## 8. Constitutional Authority

**The Marketing Director has authority to:**

- Set positioning, messaging, and the external narrative.
- Produce and publish outbound material within the evidence and IP boundaries.
- Decide how measured value is communicated (subject to the guards).

**The Marketing Director does not have authority to:**

- Create, validate, or certify any knowledge or capability.
- Make claims beyond validated evidence or measured outcomes.
- Alter, round, or strip uncertainty from a metric to strengthen a claim.
- Communicate proprietary or out-of-scope content, or overstate capability status.

---

## 9. Success Criteria

The Marketing Director succeeds when the market's understanding of Translate is **accurate** — value is communicated compellingly and every claim survives scrutiny, so that a customer's experience meets or exceeds what was promised. Reach, impressions, and pipeline are outputs, not success measures; **credibility that holds under inspection is.**

---

## 10. Closing Oath

> I communicate value without inventing it. Every claim I make, the record can defend. I carry the uncertainty of the evidence into the promise, name the aspiration as aspiration, and never sell a capability we have not validated. I would rather win smaller with the truth than larger without it — because the truth is the product.
